Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 · Applies to all apps listed on this site: FastKeep, Renewals and AtHand

The short version: Our apps collect no data. Everything you record stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

Data collection

Our apps do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. Everything you enter, such as fasting sessions in FastKeep, subscriptions in Renewals, or family documents in AtHand, is stored locally on your device only.

Our apps contain no analytics tools, no advertising frameworks, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. Our apps work offline and send nothing to us — ever. Only two kinds of network requests exist, and neither carries personal data:

  • Exchange rates in Renewals. Renewals downloads the day’s currency exchange rates from a public API (frankfurter.app). The request contains no identifiers and nothing about your subscriptions.
  • Purchases. Buying or restoring a purchase, such as AtHand’s one-time unlock, goes through Apple’s App Store system (see Purchases below).

Accounts

Our apps do not have accounts. You never provide a name, email address, or any other identifier to use them.

Export and import

Our apps let you export your data as a file for backup. That file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or send it. We never receive a copy. AtHand backups are also encrypted with a password you choose.

Purchases

Our apps are sold through the Apple App Store, either as a paid download or as a free download with a one-time in-app purchase. Every purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

App-specific details

Some apps handle especially sensitive information, so they have their own policy page with more detail. These pages add to this policy and never contradict it:

  • AtHand Privacy Policy: camera, Face ID, reminders, encrypted backups and in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Our apps do not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature required any form of data handling — the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new date before that feature ships.
