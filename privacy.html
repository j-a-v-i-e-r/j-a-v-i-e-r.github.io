Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

The short version: FastKeep collects no data. Everything you record stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

Data collection

FastKeep does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. All information you enter in the app — fasting sessions, streaks, categories, and settings — is stored locally on your device only.

FastKeep contains no analytics tools, no advertising frameworks, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. The app functions entirely offline and makes no network requests to us or anyone else.

Accounts

FastKeep does not have accounts. You never provide a name, email address, or any other identifier to use the app.

Export and import

FastKeep lets you export your data as a file for backup. That file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or send it. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

FastKeep is sold through the Apple App Store. Your purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

FastKeep does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature required any form of data handling — the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy? Email simplesapps@icloud.com.
